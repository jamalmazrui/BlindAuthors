# Blind Authors

This directory lists 59 authors who are blind or have low vision, along with selected books by each. It is meant as a resource for aspiring blind authors and as a way to find and reach the writers listed.

This is one of three companion directories, alongside [Blind Developers](https://jamalmazrui.github.io/BlindDevelopers/) and [Blind Presenters](https://jamalmazrui.github.io/BlindPresenters/).

An author is included here when every one of the following is true:

- The author was blind or had low vision at the time the book was written.
- The author is the sole author of the book. Books with any second credited author or collaborator, along with anthologies and edited collections, are not counted; an author still appears here on the strength of a separate book written alone.
- The book was first published in the year 2000 or later.
- The book is available today in a digital format, whether electronic text or audio, through a working link. A title sold only in print is not listed.

Up to three books are shown per author, newest first, each with its year where known. A poetry title must be a substantial, sole-authored collection rather than a few poems posted online. Each entry ends with a "Profiles:" line — the author's website, Amazon or National Braille Press page, LinkedIn, or Wikipedia, listed alphabetically by label — and, where one could be verified, a link to an accessible edition at the National Library Service (NLS) or Bookshare.

Authors are listed alphabetically by last name, with the books grouped by kind in an appendix.

This directory is English-language in scope: it relies on books and sources that can be read and verified in English. Blind authors writing in other languages are not represented here.
