# Blind Authors

This directory lists 42 authors who are blind or have low vision, along with selected books by each. It is meant in part as a resource for aspiring blind authors, and as a way to find and reach the writers listed.

This is one of three companion directories, alongside [Blind Developers](https://jamalmazrui.github.io/BlindDevelopers/) and [Blind Presenters](https://jamalmazrui.github.io/BlindPresenters/).

An author is included when they were blind or had low vision at the time of writing and have authored, on their own, at least one of the qualifying books; sole authorship of an available book is the bar for being listed here. The focus is contemporary: each book appeared in the year 2000 or later and is available in an electronic format through a working link, so the writing here reflects the present era and can be read today. Up to three books are shown per author, each with its year where known, followed by a "Profiles:" line giving the author's website, Amazon or National Braille Press page, LinkedIn, or Wikipedia article where one exists. Where I could verify one, a book also links to an accessible edition at the National Library Service (NLS) or Bookshare.

Authors are listed alphabetically by last name, with the books grouped by kind in an appendix.

## Authors (Alphabetical by Last Name)

### Peter Altschul {#au-altschul}
A mediator and organizational consultant.

#### [Breaking It Down and Connecting the Dots: Creating Common Ground Where Contention Rules](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B079KH2W5B) {#book-breaking-it-down}
Essays on finding common ground and working through conflict in a divided world.
Format: Kindle.

### Shelly Brisbin {#au-brisbin}
A technology journalist and podcaster.

#### [iOS Access for All](https://www.iosaccessbook.com/) {#book-ios-access-for-all}
A thorough guide to running the iPhone and iPad with VoiceOver and the other built-in accessibility features.
Format: EPUB.

### Molly Burke {#au-burke}
A content creator and public speaker, and the first blind creator to pass one million YouTube subscribers.
Profiles: [Website](https://www.mollyburkeofficial.com/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molly_Burke)

#### [Unseen: How I Lost My Vision but Found My Voice (2025)](https://www.audible.com/pd/Unseen-Audiobook/B0FG8FB1LK) {#book-unseen}
A candid memoir about identity, self-acceptance, and being seen in a world built for the sighted.
Format: Audiobook read by the author; also in print.

### Kim Charlson {#au-charlson}
The first woman president of the American Council of the Blind and director of the Perkins Braille and Talking Book Library.
Profiles: [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kim-charlson/)

#### [Drawing with Your Perkins Brailler (2009)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0999176625) {#book-drawing-perkins-brailler}
Step-by-step instructions for creating three dozen tactile pictures — shapes, animals, and seasonal themes — on a Perkins Brailler.
Format: Braille or large print.

### Ann Chiappetta {#au-chiappetta}

Profiles: [Website](http://www.annchiappetta.com)

#### [Follow Your Dog: A Story of Love and Trust](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B076RT2GKP) {#book-follow-your-dog}
A memoir about the trust and partnership between a handler and her guide dogs.

### Peggy Chong {#au-chong}
A historian and author known as The Blind History Lady.

#### [Don Mahoney: Television Star](https://www.amazon.com/Don-Mahoney-Television-Peggy-Chong/dp/1098082958) {#book-don-mahoney}
A biography of a blind entertainer who kept his blindness secret through a long television career.
Format: Also available as an ebook via Smashwords.

### Wendy S. David {#au-david}
A blind traveler and writer.

#### [Sites Unseen: Traveling the World Without Sight (2010)](https://www.nbp.org/) {#book-sites-unseen}
A travel memoir of seeing the world as a blind traveler, in the company of a series of guide dogs.
Format: National Braille Press.
