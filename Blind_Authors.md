# Blind Authors

This directory covers 30 authors who are blind or have low vision and 32 of their books, every one published in the year 2000 or later. It offers a link to one or more representative works by each author and is meant as a starting point for readers who want writing by blind people across many kinds of books: memoir and personal narrative, fiction, practical technology guides, biography, essays, and reflections on the history and culture of blindness.

A few notes on scope. The list is limited to contemporary authors, those active in the year 2000 or later, so a reader can reasonably find the work today. Unlike software, a book does not stop working with age; the practical test here is whether a title is still available in an accessible format such as Kindle, EPUB, an accessible Word document, braille, or recorded audio. Up to three books are shown for each author; where an author has written more, a link to their own page is given so the rest can be found. Where a Wikipedia article exists for an author or a book, a link to it is included as well. Several titles are also available in editions beyond the one shown, and where a book is offered free by its author or a nonprofit, that is noted.

Authors are listed alphabetically by last name. Each book title links to where the book can be obtained or learned about, so no separate link line is needed.
