# Blind Authors

This directory lists 29 authors who are blind or have low vision, along with selected books by each. It is meant in part as a resource for aspiring blind authors, and as a way to find and reach the writers listed.

An author is included when they were blind or had low vision at the time of writing. The focus is contemporary: each book appeared in the year 2000 or later and is available in an electronic format through a working link, so the writing here reflects the present era and can be read today. Up to three books are shown per author, each with its year where known, followed by a "Profiles:" line giving the author's website, Amazon or National Braille Press page, LinkedIn, or Wikipedia article where one exists.

Authors are listed alphabetically by last name, with the books grouped by kind in an appendix.

## Authors (Alphabetical by Last Name)

### Peter Altschul {#au-altschul}
A mediator and organizational consultant.

#### [Breaking It Down and Connecting the Dots: Creating Common Ground Where Contention Rules](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B079KH2W5B) {#book-breaking-it-down}
Essays on finding common ground and working through conflict in a divided world.
Format: Kindle.

### Shelly Brisbin {#au-brisbin}
A technology journalist and podcaster.

#### [iOS Access for All](https://www.iosaccessbook.com/) {#book-ios-access-for-all}
A thorough guide to running the iPhone and iPad with VoiceOver and the other built-in accessibility features.
Format: EPUB.

### Ann Chiappetta {#au-chiappetta}
A poet and memoirist.
Profiles: [Website](http://www.annchiappetta.com)

#### [Follow Your Dog: A Story of Love and Trust](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B076RT2GKP) {#book-follow-your-dog}
A memoir about the trust and partnership between a handler and her guide dogs.
Format: Kindle.

### Peggy Chong {#au-chong}
A historian and author known as The Blind History Lady.
Profiles: [Website](https://theblindhistorylady.com/), [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/stores/author/B097S7J4ML)

#### [Don Mahoney: Television Star](https://www.amazon.com/Don-Mahoney-Television-Peggy-Chong/dp/1098082958) {#book-don-mahoney}
A biography of a blind entertainer who kept his blindness secret through a long television career.
Format: Also available as an ebook via Smashwords.

### Wendy S. David {#au-david}
A blind traveler and writer.

#### [Sites Unseen: Traveling the World Without Sight (2010)](https://www.nbp.org/) {#book-sites-unseen}
A travel memoir of seeing the world as a blind traveler, in the company of a series of guide dogs.
Format: National Braille Press.

### Judy Dixon {#au-dixon}
A longtime writer on access technology.

#### [The Abundant Bookshelf: Reading Books on an iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch](https://www.nbp.org/ic/nbp/ABUNDABOOK.html) {#book-abundant-bookshelf}
A guide to finding and reading accessible books on Apple mobile devices.
Format: DOCX, National Braille Press.

### Anna Dresner {#au-dresner}
Writes accessible-technology books for National Braille Press.

#### [Computers You Can Talk To: Siri, Alexa, Google Now, and Cortana](https://www.nbp.org/ic/nbp/TALK.html) {#book-computers-you-can-talk-to}
An introduction to using voice assistants on phones, computers, and smart speakers.
Format: DOCX, National Braille Press.

### David L. Faucheux {#au-faucheux}

#### [Across Two Novembers: A Year in the Life of a Blind Bibliophile (2016)](https://www.amazon.com/ACROSS-TWO-NOVEMBERS-Blind-Bibliophile-ebook/dp/B072MCCFYT) {#book-across-two-novembers}
A year-long diary woven through with books, food, music, and memory.
Format: Kindle.

### Michael Feir {#au-feir}
A Canadian author and broadcaster who gives his books away for free.
Profiles: [Blog](https://michaelfeir.blogspot.com/)

#### [Personal Power: The iOS Edition (2025)](https://michaelfeir.blogspot.com/) {#book-personal-power}
A wide-ranging guide to getting the most from an iPhone or iPad as a totally blind user.
Format: Free ebook, third edition.

### M. Leona Godin {#au-godin}
A writer, performer, and educator.
Profiles: [Website](https://drmlgodin.com/)

#### [There Plant Eyes: A Personal and Cultural History of Blindness (2021)](https://drmlgodin.com/the-book/) {#book-there-plant-eyes}
A blend of memoir and cultural history tracing how blindness has shaped art, language, and ideas.
Format: Pantheon.

### Donna W. Hill {#au-hill}
A songwriter and novelist.

#### [The Heart of Applebutter Hill (2013)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00CNG6DDM) {#book-applebutter-hill}
A young-adult adventure novel with a blind heroine and her guide dog at its center.
Format: Kindle.

### Deborah Kendrick {#au-kendrick}
A columnist and journalist on disability.

#### [Health Care Professionals Who Are Blind or Visually Impaired](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0891283889) {#book-health-care-professionals}
Profiles of blind and low-vision people working across the health professions.
Format: Available in electronic formats, including on Bookshare.

### Deborah Kent {#au-kent}
A prolific author of fiction and nonfiction for young readers and adults.

#### [Elizabeth Blackwell: Physician and Health Educator](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00IWA94V6) {#book-elizabeth-blackwell}
A biography of the first woman to earn a medical degree in the United States.
Format: Kindle.

### Robert Kingett {#au-kingett}
A journalist.

#### [Off the Grid: Living Blind Without the Internet (2015)](https://www.amazon.com/Off-Grid-Living-Without-Internet-ebook/dp/B0FKL6B6JJ) {#book-off-the-grid}
A firsthand account of going without the internet as a blind man.
Format: Kindle.

### David Kingsbury {#au-kingsbury}
An assistive-technology instructor at the Carroll Center for the Blind.

#### [The Windows Screen Reader Primer: All the Basics and More (2024)](https://carroll.org/the-windows-screen-reader-primer-all-the-basics-and-more-third-edition) {#book-windows-primer}
A teaching guide for JAWS, NVDA, and Narrator users covering Windows, Office, browsers, and more.
Format: Free in Word and ePub.

### Stephen Kuusisto {#au-kuusisto}
A poet and University Professor at Syracuse University.
Profiles: [Website](https://bbi.syr.edu/bio/stephen-kuusisto/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Kuusisto)

#### [Have Dog, Will Travel: A Poet's Journey (2018)](https://www.amazon.com/Have-Dog-Will-Travel-Journey/dp/1476753598) {#book-have-dog-will-travel}
A lyrical memoir about independence and the bond with a first guide dog.
Format: Simon & Schuster.

### Bobbi LaChance {#au-lachance}

#### [Cobwebs (2012)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0792Y23BG) {#book-cobwebs}
A novel.
Format: Kindle.

### Andrew Leland {#au-leland}
A writer and audio producer.
Profiles: [Website](https://www.andrewleland.org/)

#### [The Country of the Blind: A Memoir at the End of Sight (2023)](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/635964/the-country-of-the-blind-by-andrew-leland/) {#book-country-of-the-blind}
A Pulitzer-finalist memoir of going blind that doubles as an exploration of the culture and history of blindness.
Format: Penguin Press.
Wikipedia: [The Country of the Blind: A Memoir at the End of Sight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Country_of_the_Blind:_A_Memoir_at_the_End_of_Sight)

### Dean Martineau {#au-martineau}
A screen-reader trainer.
Profiles: [Website](https://www.techforblind.us/)

#### [Get Cracking with Chrome for Windows](https://www.techforblind.us/get-cracking-with-chrome-for-windows/) {#book-get-cracking-chrome}
A practical guide to using the Chrome browser efficiently with a screen reader.
Format: DOCX.

### Jamal Mazrui {#au-mazrui}
Profiles: [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Jamal-Mazrui/e/B078HKV49S), [GitHub](https://github.com/jamalmazrui), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jamalmazrui/)

#### [Legacy of Mazrui: Enduring Esteem for the Man and His Work (2025)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F38NQYNJ) {#book-legacy-of-mazrui}
A tribute to the life and enduring influence of the scholar Ali Mazrui, written by his son.
Format: Kindle.

#### [Strange Truths: Objective Stories that Challenge Our Understanding of the World (2026)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GYBYZKR3) {#book-strange-truths}
An omnibus that gathers the author's Strange Truths series of accessible books on science, history, and the limits of human knowledge.
Format: Kindle.

#### [Mastery in Nonvisual Accomplishment: Among the Greatest Blind Persons of All Time (2026)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DPSQXGN9) {#book-mastery-nonvisual}
Profiles of some of the greatest blind achievers across many fields.
Format: Kindle.

### Steven B. Mendelsohn {#au-mendelsohn}
An attorney who works in disability law.

#### [People With Disabilities and Employment Law: Recent Developments, Emerging Issues, and What They Mean in Everyday Life](https://www.amazon.com/People-Disabilities-Employment-Law-Developments-ebook/dp/B01GKKY3M4) {#book-employment-law}
An overview of disability employment law and what recent changes mean in daily life.
Format: Kindle.

### Jalil Mortazavi {#au-mortazavi}

#### [The Mystery of America: What I Learned and Love About this Country](https://www.amazon.com/Mystery-America-Learned-About-Country-ebook/dp/B07B6TB66B) {#book-mystery-of-america}
Reflections from an immigrant on what he came to admire about the United States.
Format: Kindle.

### Jonathan Mosen {#au-mosen}
A broadcaster and author of many technology guides; host of the National Federation of the Blind's Access On podcast.
Profiles: [Website](https://mosen.org/)

#### [iOS Without the Eye](https://mosen.org/iosbundle/) {#book-ios-without-the-eye}
A comprehensive guide to operating the iPhone and iPad without sight.
Format: EPUB.

### James H. Omvig {#au-omvig}
A rehabilitation leader and former federal official.

#### [Freedom for the Blind: The Secret is Empowerment (2002)](https://www.amazon.com/Freedom-Blind-Empowerment-James-Omvig/dp/B0006S8FAI) {#book-freedom-for-the-blind}
An argument that strong training and high expectations are the real keys to independence.
Format: University of Arkansas Press; electronic editions available, including on Bookshare.

### Ann K. Parsons {#au-parsons}

#### [The Demmies: A Novel](https://www.amazon.com/Demmies-Novel-Ann-K-Parsons-ebook/dp/B077SH2VQK) {#book-the-demmies}
A science-fiction novel.
Format: Kindle.

### J. Pinto {#au-pinto}

#### [The Bright Side of Darkness](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0101KE0N6) {#book-bright-side-of-darkness}
A novel.
Format: Kindle.

### Abbie Johnson Taylor {#au-taylor}
A poet and memoirist.
Profiles: [Website](https://www.abbiejohnsontaylor.com/)

#### [My Ideal Partner: How I Met, Married, and Cared For the Man I Loved Despite Debilitating Odds](https://www.amazon.com/My-Ideal-Partner-Married-Debilitating-ebook/dp/B01ISO66MA) {#book-my-ideal-partner}
A memoir of love and caregiving through her husband's disabling illness.
Format: Kindle.

### Erik Weihenmayer {#au-weihenmayer}
An adventurer and athlete, the first blind person to summit Mount Everest.
Profiles: [Website](https://erikweihenmayer.com/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Weihenmayer)

#### [Touch the Top of the World: A Blind Man's Journey to Climb Farther than the Eye Can See (2001)](https://www.amazon.com/Touch-Top-World-Journey-Farther/dp/0452282942) {#book-touch-the-top}
A bestselling memoir of growing up blind and climbing toward the world's Seven Summits.
Format: Dutton.

#### [No Barriers: A Blind Man's Journey to Kayak the Grand Canyon (2017)](https://www.amazon.com/No-Barriers-Blind-Journey-Canyon/dp/125008878X) {#book-no-barriers}
A memoir of life after Everest, culminating in kayaking the length of the Grand Canyon.
Format: Thomas Dunne Books, with Buddy Levy.

### Kristen Witucki {#au-witucki}
A novelist and teacher of blind students.
Profiles: [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Kristen-Witucki/e/B00BCQVL4K)

#### [Outside Myself (2018)](https://www.amazon.com/Outside-Myself-Kristen-Witucki/dp/1942545991) {#book-outside-myself}
A novel in two voices about a blind teenage girl and an older blind man who meet through a library for the blind.
Format: Wyatt-MacKenzie.

#### [The Transcriber (2013)](https://www.amazon.com/Transcriber-Open-Door-Kristen-Witucki/dp/1936846373) {#book-the-transcriber}
A short novel narrated by the younger brother of a blind girl.
Format: GemmaMedia.
